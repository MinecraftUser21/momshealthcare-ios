The Mom’s Health Care app for iPhone and iPad (iOS 16 and later).

**Install with Sideloadly:** download `MomsHealthCare.ipa` below, then follow the
[Sideloadly steps in the README](https://github.com/MinecraftUser21/momshealthcare-ios#install-with-sideloadly).

- Book ultrasound, ECHO, gynecology, cardiology and OPD visits; the clinic calls back to confirm.
- See your appointments, add them to your calendar, cancel or book again.
- Pregnancy tracker with due date and scan timeline.
- What each service is and how to prepare.
- Opening hours, directions, one-tap call and a 112 emergency button.
- Dark, Light or Match phone.
